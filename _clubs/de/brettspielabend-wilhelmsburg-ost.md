  Hello and welcome to our board game evening!

  We're delighted that you've found your way to us. As we're just getting started, we're especially happy to welcome everyone who shares our enthusiasm for board games. Our focus is on family and strategy games for experienced players. Whether you're a casual player or already have some experience, you're very welcome to join us. If you'd like to play and meet new people, feel free to join us!

  We meet on the last Saturday of every month from 7:00 PM to 10:00 PM at Stadtteilbüro Wilhelmsburg Ost, Thielenstraße 11, 21109 Hamburg.

  We look forward to spending a fun evening full of exciting games and friendly conversations. See you at the gaming table!
