  **ABOUT US**

  The Gaming Guild is more than just a club – it is a gateway to extraordinary worlds where imagination knows no bounds and every game can be the beginning of an unforgettable adventure. We have created a welcoming space where epic adventures, strategic battles, literary discussions and a shared passion for fantasy come together to form one extraordinary story. It is a place where friendships are forged, talents flourish and everyone can become the hero of their own story.

  **OUR MISSION AND GOALS**

  Our mission is to build an inclusive community of gaming enthusiasts, fantasy lovers and people who enjoy spending their free time creatively.

  - Building a community – we bring like-minded people together, creating opportunities to forge friendships, share experiences and embark on adventures together.
  - Learning through play – we develop logical and strategic thinking, creativity, teamwork and practical skills.
  - Promoting fantasy literature and culture – we explore both classic and contemporary works, discovering extraordinary fantasy and science fiction universes.
  - An alternative to digital entertainment – we encourage face-to-face interaction, shared tabletop experiences, the development of social skills and meaningful connections.
  - Developing miniature painting and modelling skills – we nurture artistic interests while encouraging patience, precision and creativity.
  - Organising events and conventions – we create memorable experiences that bring our community together and celebrate fantasy and gaming culture.

  **WHAT WE OFFER**

  At the Gaming Guild, every game is the beginning of a new adventure!

  - Tabletop wargaming – command mighty armies, devise cunning strategies and fight spectacular battles across carefully crafted terrain.
  - Board games and card games – discover everything from timeless classics to exciting modern titles enjoyed by gamers around the world.
  - Tabletop role-playing games (TTRPGs) – step into the shoes of your chosen character and help shape extraordinary stories where every decision can change the course of events.
  - Miniature painting and modelling workshops – discover the art of painting miniatures, building scenery and creating your own battlefield terrain.
  - Discussions and shared experiences – from conversations about literature and fictional universes to in-depth explorations of game mechanics and strategies.
  - Themed events – take part in unique gatherings that transport you to distant and extraordinary worlds.

  **WHO IS IT FOR?**

  The Gaming Guild is the perfect place for you if:

  - You are looking for extraordinary adventures, exciting challenges and unforgettable experiences.
  - You want an alternative to spending time alone in front of a screen.
  - You are passionate about fantasy, literature, films or games.
  - You enjoy developing your strategic, artistic and creative skills.
  - You want to meet people who share your interests and passions.

  We welcome everyone aged 16 to 100, from experienced gamers to complete beginners taking their first steps into the hobby. Don't own any games, miniatures or cards? Unfamiliar with the rules? No problem! We provide the necessary materials, equipment and guidance from more experienced club members. All you need is curiosity, a little imagination and a willingness to join in!

  **OUR GAMING COLLECTION AND RESOURCES**

  We offer a diverse collection of games, miniatures and gaming accessories.

  - Tabletop wargames: Warhammer 40,000, Warhammer Fantasy.
  - Board games: Nemesis, The Lord of the Rings: Journeys in Middle-earth, Everdell, KLASK, Root, Blood Rage, Cthulhu: Death May Die, Cyberpunk 2077: Gangs of Night City, Dune: Imperium, Frostpunk: The Board Game, Zombicide: Green Horde, Azul: Summer Pavilion, Carcassonne, Harry Potter: Hogwarts Battle, DC Deck-Building Game, Stranger Things, Descent: Legends of the Dark, Dungeons & Dragons: Tyrants of the Underdark, Small World of Warcraft, Munchkin (various editions).
  - Miniature painting and modelling supplies – miniatures, models and materials to help you explore and develop your artistic skills.
  - Dedicated gaming space – gaming tables, battlefield terrain, miniatures and essential gaming accessories.
  - Something you won't find in any game box – great company, shared excitement, plenty of laughter and unforgettable adventures!

  **LOCATION AND ACCESSIBILITY**

  Our club is based at the Public Library of the Town and Municipality of Dębno, on the lower ground floor (Level −1). Here, in a space filled with knowledge, creativity and imagination, we come together to play, create and explore extraordinary worlds. All club meetings are completely free of charge. Simply come along and become part of our community!

  **JOIN THE GUILD!**

  Whether you are a fearless strategist, a wandering adventurer, a fantasy enthusiast, an artist or someone just discovering the world of tabletop gaming, there is a place for you in our Guild. Join a community where imagination brings people together and every game tells a new story.

  May the dice always roll in your favour! 🎲
