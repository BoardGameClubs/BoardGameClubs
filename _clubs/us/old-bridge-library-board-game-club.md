description: >-
  Join us every 2nd Sunday for an afternoon of gaming. The first 20-30 minutes are reserved for people
  to catch up, chat board games, decide on games, and welcome new attendees. Please arrive by 12:30 to
  ensure you have a seat. We play beginner games like Sushi Go!, Splendor, Carcassonne, Ticket to Ride,
  Catan and Codenames, as well as more advanced games like Wingspan and Everdell. Our typical turn-out
  is usually around 25-35 people. For those familiar with the hobby, feel free to bring your own games
  if you'd like. If you don't know how to play a game, we'll teach you! Anyone age 14 and up who is
  interested in playing great games with great people is welcome. Age 8 and up if accompanied by a
  participating adult and able to learn and play age 8+ games. If you don't use Facebook, you can also
  search the [Old Bridge Public Library Events Calendar](https://oldbridgelibrary.events.mylibrary.digital/)
  for "Modern Boardgaming Get Together".
