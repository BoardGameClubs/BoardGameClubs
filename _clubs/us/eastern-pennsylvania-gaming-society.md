  We are a club dedicated to playing games of various types - mostly tabletop board games with some miniatures and social deduction games as well. You can find lists of recently played games on our website or Meetup in the individual meeting posts.

The mall has an almost full food court with various options, and outside food is permitted, but no alcohol. Newcomers are welcome, and your first session playing is free. Discounts are available for annual or semi-annual membership.

  We were founded in 1991 and have been meeting in the greater Philadelphia area since then.
