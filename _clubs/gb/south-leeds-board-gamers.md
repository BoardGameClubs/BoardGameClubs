description: |
  A large, long-running board gaming group based in South Leeds, with players coming from across Leeds and further afield. We play everything from Catan and Ticket to Ride through to heavy euros and war games, and anyone is welcome. Plenty of lasting friendships have started at our tables.

  We meet every Tuesday from 7pm to 11pm at Bruntcliffe Working Men's Club in Morley, and it's free. There's a bar and parking on site, and a takeaway round the corner.

  Members bring games from their own collections, so you can turn up empty-handed or bring something you want to get played.

  If it's your first time, you don't need to sign up. Just walk in on a Tuesday and we'll find you a table. Plenty of the regulars are happy to teach, and there's usually a mix of lighter and heavier games going.

  We also run regular all-day events, which often bring in over 100 players from around the region.

  For news and events, join the Facebook group. The BoardGameGeek guild has members' collections, and the Discord is there if you just want a chat.
