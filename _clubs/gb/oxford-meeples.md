description: |
  A group of gamers who have been meeting in Oxford since September 2011 to play board, card, role-play and all sorts of other games (preferably with lots of meeples).

  The club meets almost every Tuesday from 7pm to 10pm in the back room of Wolvercote Village Hall: follow the path past the main entrance to the back door. Your first two nights are free. After that it's £2 a night for members and £5 for guests, and membership is £15 for the club year (September to August, reduced by £1 for each month that has passed). Please email the club before your first visit (details on their website), as space can be limited.

  Once a quarter the club also runs a Day of Gaming on a Saturday in the main hall, open to everyone. Entry is free (donations welcome) and gaming runs from around 10:30am until late. See the [Days of Gaming page](https://www.oxfordmeeples.org/days-of-gaming) for upcoming dates.
