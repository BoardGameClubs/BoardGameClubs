description: |
  Friendly group of gamers near the Kimberley area who share a passion for modern tabletop board games. All abilities welcome, happy to teach rules and offer guidance. Doors open at Nuthall Temple Centre at 6pm, the room we use is large, airy and well lit, with heating and air conditioning, with comfy chairs and big tables!

  To cover the cost of the room, we ask for £4 per person per session, however your first session with us is free! We accept cash or card. Included in your fee is free tea, coffee, fruit squash and use of the communal kitchen. There is also plenty of free parking at the venue. We also stock a small tuckshop with crisps and canned soft drinks, we ask you put something in the honesty box for anything you take. You are welcome to bring your own food, snacks and drinks, however alcohol is NOT allowed.

  Each table plays a main game for a couple of hours, then switches to lighter games until 10pm. Join the [Aftergame app group](https://aftergame.app/groups/cff1585a-3037-4c05-b405-6dce1e5b5e50) to manage attendance and claim seats at games. The group also meets at weekends at Meeples & Mulligans games shop, and runs quarterly all-day KimCon events.
