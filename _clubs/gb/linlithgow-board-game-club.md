description: |
  Founded in 2019, we’re a passionate community of locals who gather to share our love of tabletop gaming in a warm, friendly atmosphere. Whether you’re looking to make new friends, escape the house for an evening, or give your brain an exciting workout, you’ve found your tribe.

  We have regular Saturday evening gaming sessions, plus we host a couple of extended gaming marathons throughout the year for those who want to dive deeper into longer, more complex games.

  We also have a thriving Dungeons & Dragons group for those who enjoy role-playing adventures.

  Our doors are open to everyone – from curious newcomers taking their first steps into modern board gaming to seasoned strategists with years of experience under their belts.

  Our game library spans the spectrum of modern tabletop gaming. You’ll find beloved classics like Carcassonne, Ticket to Ride, and Splendor alongside more complex strategy games such as Concordia and Great Western Trail.

  Feel free to bring your own games to share, join in with what others are playing, or browse our growing club library to discover something new.

  We alternate weeks between Board Games and RPGs. Entry is free, but please support the cafe.
